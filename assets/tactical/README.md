# Original tactical artwork and character voices

The character pose sheets and battlefield backgrounds were generated for this fictional educational game using Google Flow Nano Banana Pro. Transparent character exports were prepared with the built-in image-generation editing tool, then cropped and visually checked. The six poses are distinct generated still poses; game movement interpolates these poses rather than claiming a hand-animated frame sequence.

The primary squad is Ying-Cen (guardian), Ruo-Lan (scout), and Cheng-Ze (medic). Their initial appearances and synthetic voice styles are character choices, not restrictions on professions or player identity. Opponents are fictional training machines. No real insignia, blood, or copied game characters are used.

Chinese audio is original dialogue rendered with Google AI Studio stock synthetic voices. It does not imitate a real person. The build-time verification manifest records exact-transcript audio-model checks, decode/loudness/clipping checks, and the limitation that no human subjective listening review was performed. Do not describe these assets as human voice-actor recordings or as independently verified accent quality.

The existing thirteen original Web Audio chapter scores remain in use. Source sheets and production audit files are excluded from the published Pages artifact. No student identity, reflection, textbook PDF, or classroom record belongs in this asset directory.
